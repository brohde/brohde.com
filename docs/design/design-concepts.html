Studio recommendation

Develop 10 — Detection. It's the only direction where the aesthetic *is* the argument: a potential client lands on the site and immediately understands that this person ships computer-vision products, before reading a word. It's distinctive without a single stock photo, scales to an image-light homepage, and the motif deepens naturally in the Eight Angle deep-dive (real annotated screenshots). Its risk — HUD kitsch — is a restraint problem, and restraint is executable.

Keep 02 — Index as the runner-up (quietest concept that still signals engineering precision; lowest build cost; safest with conservative corporate clients), and consider a blend: Index's ledger structure as the skeleton, Detection's brackets/confidence chips reserved for work articles only — off-topic travel posts would drop the CV grammar entirely, which also solves the category-separation question elegantly.

If you want dark: 08 build.log is the strongest dark identity, but it's also the most common trope in dev portfolios; I'd rather see its log-entry date format adopted inside Index or Detection than run it whole.

How to respond: name a winner, a shortlist of 2–3 to push further, or elements to blend ("Detection's chips on Index's grid"). I'll then have the design team develop the pick into a full system — article page, about, tokens, motion spec — before any build.
