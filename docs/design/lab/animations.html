Six ways a homepage can breathe

You liked the constellation. Here it is again as the reference point, plus five wildly different ambient techniques. Page design is ignored on purpose: each tile is just the animation, with sample copy on top to prove the text always wins.

House rules, applied to all six: devicePixelRatio capped at 2 · frame rates throttled to 30fps or lower · every animation pauses offscreen and on hidden tabs · prefers-reduced-motion gets a composed still, with a play button to opt back in · palettes here are per-demo placeholders, any of these recolors to the chosen design system.

01

Constellation

The reference point: a drifting proximity mesh, every article a node in the network.

Brian Rohde Full-stack engineer shipping AI products end to end

canvas 2dAbout 40 seeded nodes, an n² proximity pass, links fade with distance. Well under 1ms a frame at the 30fps cap. Reduced motion: the seeded layout draws once as a still constellation.

02

Aurora

Your seed idea: blurred color fields drift and fade over ink. Pure CSS, zero JavaScript per frame.

Brian Rohde Full-stack engineer shipping AI products end to end

pure cssFour blurred radial gradients on compositor-only transform and opacity keyframes, 47s to 82s loops, SVG grain to kill banding. No main-thread work at all. Reduced motion: the keyframes hold on their opening composition.

03

Ink Drift

A few hundred particles ride an invisible flow field, leaving slow ink-wash trails on paper.

Brian Rohde Full-stack engineer shipping AI products end to end

canvas 2dRoughly 250 particles advected through a layered-sine flow field; trails come from a 4.5% alpha wash and two batched strokes per frame, about 1ms at 30fps. Reduced motion: the field is pre-rolled 240 steps into a finished ink wash.

04

Isolines

A live topographic map: contour lines of a slowly shifting field, like weather moving over terrain.

Brian Rohde Full-stack engineer shipping AI products end to end

canvas 2dMarching squares over a roughly 90x45 scalar field, 15 contour levels, throttled to 25fps, about 2ms per frame. Every fourth line is a brighter index contour, the cartography convention. Reduced motion: one evolved frame, a still topographic plate.

05

Detection Pass

A survey line sweeps the page, brackets what it finds, scores it, lets it go. A nod to the Eight Angle pose model.

Brian Rohde Full-stack engineer shipping AI products end to end

canvas 2d + css dot gridOne sweep every 10.7s; targets are seeded away from the copy zone, ignite as the line crosses them, then decay to ghost outlines. Near-zero cost between ignitions. Reduced motion: frozen mid-pass with two detections lit.

06

Glyph Tide

A terminal raster: waves roll through a grid of characters, density rendered as type. The 8 is not an accident.

Brian Rohde Full-stack engineer shipping AI products end to end

canvas 2d text rasterAbout 1,700 cells redrawn at 9fps; the slow tick is the aesthetic, so the throttle costs nothing. Roughly 2ms per tick. Reduced motion: a single settled frame.
