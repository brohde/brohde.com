## What demo 04 is

The composed homepage scene: glyph **tide canvas UNDER the aurora blob layers, glass terminal on top**. That layer order is the core trick: translucent blurred blobs tint the glyphs in the compositor for free, so every color change (state, theme) recolors the tide with zero per-cell JavaScript. Since the rework, **a page theme is a full-scene identity: one press changes sky, ground, glass, tide peak and the source mark together** on one 2.6s clock. Three tide placements audition against the full page: full sky (capped murmur), right gutter (full voice, studio pick for homepage), hero band (studio pick for article pages).

## Mechanisms (all in the one file's `glyph4` IIFE + stage 4 CSS)

- **Theme press owns the scene** (`setTheme4()`): one press stamps the incoming sky slot, eases ground + glass + well under a transient `.theming` class (`transition: background-color 2600ms ease` on the stage, `.term`, `.prompt`, `.stagetag`; class removed after ~2700ms so ground toggles and placement switches stay instant), lerps the tide peak (`glyph4.setTheme`), and travels the source mark (`glyph4.request(THEME_SOURCE[name])`). `THEME_SOURCE = { default: eight, eight: logo, news: f, android: g }`: the tide draws what the page is. `applyGround()` calls `snapThemeFade()` first: a mid-flight theme fade must never slow a ground swap, because the text inks snap with `.lite`, so the floor has to snap with them.
- **Two-slot sky crossfade**: the sky lives in two stacked `.skyslot` divs; a theme change stamps the incoming slot's `data-sky` (scoped custom properties in CSS) and crossfades opacity over **2.6s**, the same clock as the home↔chat warm/cool morph.
- **Tide peak lerp**: the 3 ink buckets are one calm shared text ramp (home/chat lerped); the 4th "peak" bucket is the page theme's voice. `PEAKS[theme][ground][home|chat]`; theme travel (`tmix`) reroutes gracefully mid-flight by freezing current interpolated peaks as the new start. State tint (`mix`) and theme travel both ease on the 2.6s clock.
- **Source travel, never swap**: envelope `m` goes out to pure tide over 1.4s, holds 0.9s, forms the next source over 1.4s (`request()`/`advance()`; auto-cycle dwell 4.6s). The theme press routes the mark through this same envelope: out through pure tide while the old sky dissolves, forming again as the new sky settles. The source row stays live as a manual override for auditioning. Reduced motion: `settle()` renders a still instantly.
- **Contain-fit sampling** (`sampleOne`): every 480×300 source contain-fits the tide area, centered both axes, computed in stage pixels and mapped back to the non-square cell grid (15×18px cells); the white letterbox samples to zero = pure tide. Sources re-sampled once per resize into `LUM`, never per tick.
- **Sources are procedural** (lab rule: no external assets): `drawSrcEight` (the 8), `drawSrcLogo` (Eight Angle mark traced bold from `assets/images/case-studies/eight-angle/hero-live-2026-07.png`: the A, hip ring, big O), `drawSrcPose` (astavakrasana silhouette), `drawSrcLetter` f/G stand-ins (real brand marks pending trademark check, #45/#59). Production swaps `drawImage(asset)` through the same seam.
- **Two capped strengths** (`eff()`), both where the tide lives behind the whole page: the phone background fallback (below 700px with gutter placement, CSS drops the mask, the canvas goes full-bleed, `setBgCap(true)`) holds whisper at **0.12**; the full-sky placement (`setSkyCap(true)`) holds a murmur at **0.28**, because its glass pours lighter and the worst-overlap AA numbers are measured at exactly that pairing: lighter glass buys visibility, the cap pays for it. `applyAlpha()` bottoms canvas opacity near a third of full strength (dark-on-light texture carries more edge contrast, so light ground needs the deeper cut).
- **Placement defaults**: `PLACE_DEFAULT = { sky: 26, gutter: 70, hero: 60 }`; slider stays live after selection.
- **Glass**: built from `--glass-rgb` + `--glass-a` so themes tint it. Base pours `0.78` night / `0.8` light (stronger than demo 01 because a picture lives under it); **the full-sky placement lightens the pour to `0.66` night / `0.74` light** so the tide ghosts through the terminal. The input well derives from the same rgb (alpha `0.55` night / `0.72` light), so it retints with the theme too; the terminal never sits green on a blue night. Stage-scoped ink overrides: night `--t-dim: #A2B8AB`, light `--t-tag: #7C440C` (see audit). Standing rule: **text only ever sits on glass, never on bare tide**.
- **Shared runner**: `wire()` + IntersectionObserver; 9fps tide (interval 110ms), pauses offscreen/hidden/user-paused/reduced-motion. dpr capped at 2.

## Theme identities as shipped (skies in `.skyslot[data-sky]`, grounds + glass tints in `#stage4[data-theme]`, peaks in JS `PEAKS`)

- **Grounds are part of the identity**: eight angle goes teal-black night `#0A1716` + the brand cream `#F6F3EE` by day; newsroom goes deep Facebook-blue night `#0C1B36` + blue-cast paper `#F2F6FD` (the page reads blue before a single blob drifts past); android deliberately keeps a neutral graphite floor `#101216` night / `#F6F7F9` light: four brand hues on one floor read as a toy box, so the quartet sky carries the color instead. Specificity note: the themed ground blocks sit after the `.lite` blocks so night themed values beat the plain lite ground, and lite themed values beat both.
- **default**: violet/teal/indigo home, amber/gold/coral chat; peaks mint `#7EE787`→gold `#F5B968` night, deep green `#086148`→brown `#8A4D0F` light. The 8 is default's mark only.
- **eight**: brand teal family (lead blob `#40C4B0` night), cream `#F6F3EE` carried into the light ground; peaks `#46C8AE`→`#EFC272` night, `#0E7263`→`#8A6428` light.
- **news**: Facebook blue `#1877F2` family; peaks luminous `#7AA7FF`→gold `#F3C77B` night, deep `#1D4ED8`→`#8A5A0F` light.
- **android**: full Google quartet re-weighted by state (blue+green lead cold home, yellow+red lead warm chat) so it never reads as all four at once; the cold home sky pours a little stronger because the ground stays neutral; peaks `#3DDC84`→`#FCC934` night, `#188038`→`#9A6207` light.
- **Rose anchor rule**: blob 4 (rose) is the continuity thread, never overridden by themes; only overriding blobs 1-3 is structural. Exception: android trades rose for the quartet (shipped in the approved delivery).
- **Chat stays golden hour under every theme** by design: page identity lives in the reading state; the warm chat sky only takes the theme's cast, so moving between pages never changes what talking feels like.

## Audit + perf methods (repeat these after any visual change)

- **Contrast**: the audit gained two axes this round: the themed grounds + glass tints, and the two glass pours (the poured 0.78/0.8 that gutter and hero keep, the lighter 0.66/0.74 full-sky pour with its strength cap). It still sweeps every theme × both grounds × both states × six backdrop candidates per combo: peak glyph and dense ink mass, each with all four blobs stacked at center alpha, each bare, plus blobs alone and bare ground, all under the glass. Checker script pattern: `scratchpad/contrast04-themes.js` (session-scoped scratchpad; rewrite if gone). The lighter sky glass moved the worst frame: no longer the bare night peak but the **warm chat peak under the full blob stack, seen through 34% transmission**. Two inks moved to hold the line, both scoped to stage 4: night secondary `#8FA79A`→`#A2B8AB` (the old value bottomed at 3.9:1 under the newsroom chat sky; now 4.7:1 there, 4.9:1 under default and eight angle, 5.5:1 under android, 5.7:1 at the old worst spot) and light chat tag `#8A4D0F`→`#7C440C` (the eight angle cream glass shaved the old brown to a 4.5:1 edge; now 5.3:1 at worst). The corner tag plate takes the theme's glass tint and measures 6.6:1 or better. Shipped worst across every theme, ground, state, pour and backdrop: **4.7:1**. All AA+, body inks AAA.
- **Perf**: headless Chrome, 1034×618 full sky at full strength (~2,400 cells, slider pinned at 100), a deliberately busy 22 seconds: four theme changes each carrying a ground ease and a mark travel, one manual source travel, a state recolor, a ground toggle, two placement switches; dpr 1 and 2. Shipped: dpr 1 avg 0.73ms/tick, p95 0.9ms, max 1.2ms over 157 ticks; dpr 2 avg 0.77ms, p95 1.1ms, max 1.4ms, vs the 4ms budget: the worst tick uses about a third of it. Zero runtime errors; the same scenario under forced reduced motion runs zero ticks. Nothing a theme change does touches the tick: the sky swap is compositor opacity on the two slots, the ground/glass recolor is one background-color ease on solid rectangles, and the mark travel is the same envelope the source buttons drive.

## Taste calls approved by Brian (2026-07-15, "I'm good with this!")

The reworked delivery shipped with four flagged taste calls; Brian accepted all four as delivered:

1. Stage 4's night secondary sits one step lighter than demo 01's (`#A2B8AB` vs `#8FA79A`): the full-sky glass pours lighter here and the old value cannot hold 4.5:1 through 34% transmission. It stays clearly quieter than the body ink.
2. Full sky is a capped placement by design: strength held at 0.28 however far the slider goes; lighter glass buys visibility, the cap pays for it.
3. The android home sky pours stronger than its chat sky: the neutral graphite ground carries no color, so the cold quartet sky compensates.
4. The newsroom night ground `#0C1B36` rides a shade brighter than the other night floors so the blue registers as blue, not black.

## Still open (variants, not blockers)

1. Landscape 480×300 sources present small in the tall gutter; portrait/square per-page assets would present larger.
2. Newsroom-blue chat instead of golden hour stays a one-line palette change if ever wanted; golden-hour chat shipped as the standing rule.
3. Android pair-split variant could keep the rose anchor if continuity ever outweighs the quartet.
4. Phone fallback deliberately near-invisible on light ground; cap can rise to ~0.2 with a contrast re-measure.

## Production wiring notes

Placement, theme, ground and source are per-page (a class, a palette, an asset); state follows the route. A theme is a full-scene identity (#74): a production page carries its ground, glass tint, sky palette, peak and mark as one package. Integration is decision #59's phase (after Option C), governed by the facade contract (#70): server-rendered/static facade first, tide inits at idle/visible, zero pre-paint bytes.
