Alternatives to Squarespace

375px frame · date column folds above the title

What changed

  • Cream is out. Warm cream plus high-contrast serif plus oxblood is the most recognizable AI-generated look of 2026; the ground is now gallery white with cool ink, which reads printed rather than prompted.
  • The accent is a plain editorial link blue, used for links and nothing else. Generated palettes studiously avoid default-looking blue; human editors never have. Links look like links.
  • Serif and sans split the work. Newsreader keeps titles only; masthead, nav, dates, and the intro move to a quiet grotesk. The division of labor is an editorial convention machines rarely pick.
  • The italic serif intro is gone, the single most common AI-editorial tell.
  • Kept, per Brian: margin-hung dates, the list structure, hairline rules, the calm, and the grow-on-hover underline (now blue).

Type

Newsreader (titles only) + Inter (interface and prose)

Shown in Charter / system sans

Palette

#FFFFFF #17181A #2242C8 #5B5E63

On white: ink 17.8:1 · gray 6.5:1 · link blue 7.8:1. All pass 4.5:1.

Motion

Unchanged from round 1: rows fade-rise on scroll with a 60ms stagger; underlines grow left→right; both disabled under reduced motion.

Signature

Dates hung in the margin, like a printed quarterly's folios.

Open questions for Brian

  1. Circular files: which LL Circular weights does the license cover (Book, Medium, Bold, Black), and when can we expect the woff2 files? Avenir Next stands in until then; Inter is the interim webfont if Circular slips past build start.
  2. Exposed Grid title face: A (Inter Tight), B (IBM Plex Mono), or C (Times New Roman)? Studio leans A for client credibility; C is the most memorable and costs zero font bytes.
  3. Quarterly accent: does the plain link blue land, or would you rather go fully monochrome (black ink only, underlines as the sole link affordance)?
  4. Conversational layer: is the chat prompt a real roadmap item? If yes, it affects architecture (an API endpoint on Cloudflare, likely a small LLM route) and should be scoped before the build, even if it ships after launch.
  5. Dash policy: em and en dashes are now banned in all design copy on this page; VOICE.md still lists em dashes as "confirm with Brian" for article prose. Recommend logging the ban as global in DECISIONS.md.
  6. Which direction advances to a full system pass (article page, about page, tokens, motion spec)? One winner or a final two-way bake-off.
